Title: Attention Is All You Need

Abstract
The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks. We propose the Transformer, a model architecture eschewing recurrence and relying entirely on attention mechanisms.

1. Introduction
Sequence modeling has been dominated by RNNs. The Transformer addresses long-range dependency problems with self-attention.

2. Method
We use multi-head attention, positional encoding, and stacked encoder-decoder layers. Each layer applies scaled dot-product attention followed by feed-forward networks.

3. Experiments
We evaluate on WMT 2014 English-to-German and English-to-French translation tasks. Our model achieves new state-of-the-art BLEU scores while being more parallelizable.

4. Results
The Transformer base model outperforms previous best results on both translation benchmarks with significantly less training cost.

5. Conclusion
Attention-only architectures are effective for sequence transduction and offer strong performance with improved training efficiency.
