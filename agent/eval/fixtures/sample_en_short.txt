Title: BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers

Abstract
We introduce BERT, which stands for Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. Unlike previous models, BERT is designed to pre-train deep bidirectional representations from unlabeled text.

1. Introduction
Language model pre-training has improved many NLP tasks. BERT uses masked language modeling and next sentence prediction.

2. Method
BERT uses a multi-layer bidirectional Transformer encoder. We pre-train on BooksCorpus and English Wikipedia with masked LM and NSP objectives.

3. Experiments
We fine-tune BERT on GLUE, SQuAD, and SWAG. Hyperparameters include batch size 256, Adam with learning rate 1e-4, and 1M training steps.

4. Results
BERT-base and BERT-large outperform prior state-of-the-art on eleven NLP tasks, including large gains on SQuAD and GLUE benchmarks.

5. Conclusion
Bidirectional pre-training enables strong transfer learning for downstream NLP applications.
